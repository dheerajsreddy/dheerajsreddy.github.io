Sarcasm Detection in Low-Resource Indic Languages

Sarcasm is hard for models even in English; in code-mixed Hindi and Tamil there is barely any labelled data. I fine-tuned XLM-RoBERTa over 23,000 tweets and beat the standard Indic baselines.

  • Macro F1 0.77 → 0.91 against mBERT and IndicBERT (+18%)
  • RandomOverSampler to handle severe class imbalance

About

I like the part of the job where a vague idea has to become something that actually stays up.

At Scry AI that has meant authentication, a sandboxed parser system that has to safely run untrusted code, and LLM pipelines that chew through hundreds of documents without falling over. Before that, at Netcore Unbxd, it meant keeping a recommendation system consistent while events flew between services.

The thread through all of it is that I never stopped being interested in the model itself. My degrees are in machine intelligence and computer science — USC for the master's, PES University for the bachelor's, where I specialised in Machine Intelligence & Data Science and held a merit scholarship for the top 20% of my class. I have published on multimodal emotion recognition, pushed sarcasm detection in Indic languages, and built agents for fun on weekends.

So: an engineer who ships, who happens to know exactly what is going on inside the model you are shipping.
